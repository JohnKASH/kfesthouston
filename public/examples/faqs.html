General
Do I need a ticket to enter? +
No ticket needed! Entry to Korean Festival Houston is completely free and open to the public.
Where exactly is the festival? +
KFest is held at Discovery Green, 1500 McKinney St, Houston, TX 77010 — right in the heart of Downtown Houston.
Will the festival be cancelled if it rains? +
Houston weather can be unpredictable! If anything changes, we'll notify everyone via social media and our website. Check @kfesthtx before heading out.
Planning Your Visit
Is there parking nearby? +
Multiple parking garages are available near Discovery Green. We highly recommend taking MetroRail — the Downtown Transit Center is steps away. Rideshare drop-off is also easy.
Are there special hotel rates for out-of-town visitors? +
We highly recommend contacting the Hilton Americas-Houston for rates during festival weekend.
Is the festival kid-friendly? +
Absolutely! K-Fest has activities and performances for all ages. The Korean Village features crafts, games, and story readings great for families.
Can I bring my pet? +
Yes! Discovery Green is a pet-friendly park. Leashed pets are welcome at the festival.
Accessibility
Is there a sensory-friendly option? +
Yes! Sunday, October 11 features Sensory Friendly hours from 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM with reduced noise levels, making it easier for attendees with invisible disabilities to enjoy the festival comfortably.
Food & Payment
Do vendors accept cards and cash? +
Yes! Our vendors accept both cash and cards for your convenience.
Sponsorship & Partnerships
How can my company become a sponsor? +
We'd love to partner with you! Send your sponsorship inquiry to info@kashouston.org and our team will get back to you.
